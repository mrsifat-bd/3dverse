Files: /models/skeleton-hi.glb, /models/skeleton-lo.glb, /models/skeleton-front.webp

These files are an adaptation of:

  Z-Anatomy — The libre 3D atlas of anatomy — CC-BY-SA 4.0
  https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy
  which is based on
  BodyParts3D — The Database Center for Life Science (DBCLS)
  (listed by Z-Anatomy as CC-BY-SA 2.1 Japan; DBCLS now publishes BodyParts3D
  under CC Attribution 4.0 International)
  https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/

Changes made by 3D Verse BD (November 2026):
  - Only the skeletal system was used. Muscles, organs, nerves, cartilages of
    the larynx and nose, auditory ossicles, sinuses, text labels and every
    non-commercial Z-Anatomy component (inner ear, kidney) were removed.
  - Teeth were merged into the maxillae and the mandible; hand bones and foot
    bones were merged into one object per side; objects were renamed.
  - Meshes were simplified (decimated), quantised and compressed (meshopt).
